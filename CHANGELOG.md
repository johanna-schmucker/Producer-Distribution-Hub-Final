# Landing page change log

Producer Distribution Hub, Team Amsterdam, 11000A Entrepreneurship & Innovation.
Each entry says what changed and the insight behind it. Sources are the team's validation interviews (as summarised in
our pitch), feedback from team members, and the A6 landing page instructions.

## Insights from our validation interviews that shaped the page

| What we heard | What changed on the page |
|---|---|
| Shoppers we interviewed care where their food comes from and when it was picked. | Headline "a farmer's name on the box", QR traceability shown in the farm-to-plate graphic, section "Every plate has a farm behind it". |
| A small farm we interviewed said selling to restaurants and big markets is hard because there are only two people. | Section "Close the gap" and the farms tab: we handle ordering, invoicing and delivery so farms can keep growing. |
| Grocers do not want to chase a dozen small suppliers with a dozen invoices. | Promise of one order, one invoice, one delivery, shown in the hero example order and the graphic. |
| Buyers and farms need to know the terms before they join. | New "Business model" tab: $0 upfront for farms, about 18% commission, farms paid within 14 days, labelled as planned pilot terms. |
